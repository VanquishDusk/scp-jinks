About

SCP: JINKS is a single-player retro voxel SCP Foundation facility simulation. Work a shift at Site-██ as Class-D personnel or as a security guard: take assignments from the staff around you, earn Credits, and survive when containment fails.

This game is silent by design: it has no audio.

Based on the SCP Foundation (scp-wiki.wikidot.com), licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. The SCP emblem is used under the same license. Rendering by three.js (MIT).
